Privacy Policy  ·  Politika privatnosti

Last updated: 21 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Stickers Master mobile application ("the app") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The app is developed and operated by Ljubomir Maksimović ("we", "us"), an individual developer, acting as the data controller. For any privacy question or request, contact us at stickers.master26@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

Stickers Master helps collectors track their sticker album progress, find other collectors in their city to swap duplicates with, and message each other once both sides agree. Some features work without an account; swapping and messaging require signing in.

3. Information we collect

Account information

  • When you first open the app, an anonymous account with a randomly generated identifier is created so your collection can be saved. This contains no personal information.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive from Google your name, email address, profile photo, and Google account identifier, and store these as part of your account.

Collection data

  • Which stickers you mark as owned, and how many duplicates you have.

Profile and swap information (optional)

  • If you choose to take part in the swap area, you provide a display name, city, and country. Your profile photo (from your Google account) may also be shown.
  • The list of sticker codes you have available to swap.

Messages and safety data

  • Chat requests and the messages you send, including their text and timestamps.
  • Records of users you block, and any abuse reports you submit (including the reason and any details you add).

Technical data

  • A push-notification token for your device, used to deliver notifications about chat requests and messages. This is created only for signed-in (non-anonymous) accounts.
  • App settings such as your chosen language and theme, stored locally on your device.

Device permissions

  • Notifications — used to alert you about new chat requests and messages. You can decline or revoke this at any time in your device settings.
  • Camera — used only for the optional "page scan" feature, where you photograph an album page as a visual aid while marking stickers. These photos are used only on your device and are not uploaded to or stored by us.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing activity.

4. How we use your information

  • To save and synchronize your sticker collection across your devices.
  • To match you with nearby collectors and show your profile in the swap area, if you opt in.
  • To deliver chat requests and messages between you and other users.
  • To operate the block and report tools and keep the community safe.
  • To send notifications you have permitted.

5. What other users can see

If you take part in the swap area, your display name, city, country, and profile photo are visible to other signed-in users. When you send a chat request or message, the recipient can see your name, photo, and the content you send. Your email address is never shown to other users.

6. Legal bases for processing

Where data-protection law (including the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation and equivalent laws in the region) applies, we rely on: performance of our service to you (providing the app's features); your consent (for optional features such as the swap profile and notifications); and our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and functional.

7. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for advertising. The app's backend runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, and Cloud Functions), provided by Google. Google acts as our processor and stores the data on our behalf. Signing in uses Google Sign-In. Google's handling of data is governed by Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

8. Where your data is stored

Account, collection, profile, and message data is stored using Google Firebase in a data centre located in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

9. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your data is removed as described in the next section. Abuse reports may be retained as long as needed to keep the service safe.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can:

  • Edit your profile information at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account and data directly in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, collection, profile, swap data, chats, and related records.
  • Contact us at stickers.master26@gmail.com to make any other request. We will respond within a reasonable time.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Where local law sets a higher age of digital consent, that age applies. A child who uses the app should do so only with the involvement of a parent or guardian, and should not sign in with a personal account or share personal details. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access is restricted by security rules so that each user can reach only their own data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will change, and significant changes will be communicated within the app where appropriate.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: stickers.master26@gmail.com.

Ova Politika privatnosti objašnjava kako mobilna aplikacija Stickers Master („aplikacija“) prikuplja, koristi i štiti vaše podatke. Korišćenjem aplikacije prihvatate ovde opisane prakse.

1. Ko je odgovoran za vaše podatke

Aplikaciju razvija i njome upravlja Ljubomir Maksimović („mi“, „nas“), kao individualni programer i rukovalac podacima. Za svako pitanje ili zahtev u vezi sa privatnošću, kontaktirajte nas na stickers.master26@gmail.com.

2. Šta aplikacija radi

Stickers Master pomaže kolekcionarima da prate napredak u popunjavanju albuma sa sličicama, pronađu druge kolekcionare u svom gradu za razmenu duplikata i razmenjuju poruke kada se obe strane saglase. Neke funkcije rade bez naloga; razmena i poruke zahtevaju prijavu.

3. Podaci koje prikupljamo

Podaci o nalogu

  • Kada prvi put otvorite aplikaciju, kreira se anonimni nalog sa nasumično generisanim identifikatorom kako bi vaša kolekcija mogla da se sačuva. On ne sadrži nikakve lične podatke.
  • Ako odaberete da se prijavite putem Google naloga, od Google-a dobijamo vaše ime, adresu e-pošte, profilnu fotografiju i identifikator Google naloga, i čuvamo ih kao deo vašeg naloga.

Podaci o kolekciji

  • Koje sličice ste označili da posedujete i koliko duplikata imate.

Podaci o profilu i razmeni (opciono)

  • Ako odlučite da učestvujete u delu za razmenu, unosite prikazno ime, grad i državu. Može biti prikazana i vaša profilna fotografija (sa vašeg Google naloga).
  • Spisak šifara sličica koje imate dostupne za razmenu.

Poruke i bezbednosni podaci

  • Zahtevi za ćaskanje i poruke koje šaljete, uključujući njihov tekst i vreme slanja.
  • Evidencija o korisnicima koje blokirate i o prijavama zloupotrebe koje podnesete (uključujući razlog i sve detalje koje dodate).

Tehnički podaci

  • Token za potisne notifikacije vašeg uređaja, koji se koristi za isporuku obaveštenja o zahtevima i porukama. Kreira se samo za prijavljene (ne-anonimne) naloge.
  • Podešavanja aplikacije, kao što su izabrani jezik i tema, koja se čuvaju lokalno na vašem uređaju.

Dozvole uređaja

  • Notifikacije — koriste se da vas obaveste o novim zahtevima i porukama. Možete ih odbiti ili opozvati u bilo kom trenutku u podešavanjima uređaja.
  • Kamera — koristi se isključivo za opcionu funkciju „skeniranje stranice“, gde fotografišete stranicu albuma kao vizuelnu pomoć pri označavanju sličica. Te fotografije se koriste samo na vašem uređaju i ne šalju se nama niti ih mi čuvamo.

Aplikacija ne sadrži nikakve reklame, analitičke alate niti pratioce trećih strana. Ne prikupljamo vašu lokaciju, kontakte ni aktivnost pregledanja.

4. Kako koristimo vaše podatke

  • Da sačuvamo i sinhronizujemo vašu kolekciju sličica između vaših uređaja.
  • Da vas povežemo sa kolekcionarima u blizini i prikažemo vaš profil u delu za razmenu, ako se na to odlučite.
  • Da isporučimo zahteve za ćaskanje i poruke između vas i drugih korisnika.
  • Da omogućimo alate za blokiranje i prijavljivanje i očuvamo bezbednost zajednice.
  • Da šaljemo notifikacije koje ste dozvolili.

Ako učestvujete u delu za razmenu, vaše prikazno ime, grad, država i profilna fotografija vidljivi su drugim prijavljenim korisnicima. Kada pošaljete zahtev za ćaskanje ili poruku, primalac vidi vaše ime, fotografiju i sadržaj koji šaljete. Vaša adresa e-pošte nikada se ne prikazuje drugim korisnicima.
