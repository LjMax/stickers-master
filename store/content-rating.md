# Stickers Master — Content Rating Questionnaire

Guidance for the Play Console content-rating section (App content → Content rating).
The questionnaire is run by the IARC (International Age Rating Coalition) and produces
ratings for every region automatically once you answer honestly. You only answer
questions; the rating is generated for you.

Exact question wording in the Console may differ slightly from the headings below, but
the substance — and therefore the correct answer — is what matters.

---

## Step 1 — Email address
The Console asks for a contact email for the rating authority.
→ Use **stickers.master26@gmail.com** (same address as the privacy policy).

## Step 2 — App category
Choose the **non-game application** category (often shown as "Reference, News, or
Educational" / "Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other"). Stickers Master is a
utility/social app, not a game.
→ Pick the general **app / utility** category, not a game category.

---

## Step 3 — Content questions

### Violence
- Realistic or cartoon violence? → **No**
- Depictions of blood, gore, injury? → **No**

### Sexuality
- Nudity or sexual content? → **No**
- Sexual innuendo or references? → **No**

### Language
- Profanity or crude humour? → **No**
- (The app's own text contains none. User chat messages are covered under the
  user-interaction questions below, not here.)

### Controlled substances
- References to or depiction of drugs, alcohol, or tobacco? → **No**

### Gambling
- Simulated gambling? → **No**
- Real-money gambling? → **No**
- Contests or sweepstakes? → **No**

### Fear / mature themes
- Content likely to frighten or disturb? → **No**

---

## Step 4 — Interactive elements (the part that actually applies)

This is where Stickers Master has "yes" answers. These don't add objectionable
content — they add interaction descriptors to the rating.

- **Do users interact or communicate with each other?** → **Yes**
  (Request-gated 1:1 chat plus the swap area.)

- **Can users share user-generated content with each other?** → **Yes**
  (Chat messages and the short chat-request intro message.)

- **Are there tools to moderate / report / block other users?** → **Yes**
  (The app has block, report, and a 24-hour re-request cooldown. Mention these if the
  questionnaire asks how user interaction is managed — it counts in your favour.)

- **Does the app share the user's location with other users?** → **Yes**
  (The swap area shows each collector's city to others. **Judgment call — see notes.**)

- **Does the app let users buy digital content / contain in-app purchases?** → **No**
  (The app is free, no IAP, no ads.)

- **Does the app contain ads?** → **No**

---

## Step 5 — Expected outcome

With no violence, sex, language, substances, or gambling, and only the user-interaction
and location-sharing elements answered "yes", the generated rating will be a **low age
rating** (e.g. ESRB Everyone, PEGI 3, USK 0) **with content descriptors** noting that
**users can interact** and that the app **shares location**. That is the correct,
expected result for a collector-swap app with chat — it is not a problem.

---

## Notes — judgment calls

1. **"Shares location" (Step 4).** The location shared is only a city, and it is typed
   by the user, not sensed by GPS. Still, it is the user's location and it is visible
   to other users in the swap area, so the honest answer is **Yes**. This also keeps
   the questionnaire consistent with the Data Safety form, where the city is declared
   as "Approximate location". If the exact Console question says "current" or "precise"
   location, the answer would be **No** — read the wording carefully and match it.

2. **Re-rating on changes.** If you later add features (e.g. image sharing in chat,
   purchases), you must re-do the questionnaire — content ratings are not automatically
   updated.

---

## Related — Target audience (separate Console section)

Content rating and "Target audience and content" are two different sections, but they
interact. Because Stickers Master has open user-to-user chat, **do not include an
under-13 age group** in the target-audience section. Targeting children would pull the
app into Google Play's Families policy, which restricts open chat and user-generated
content and would require significant extra compliance work. Set the target audience to
**13+ / teen and adult** age groups only.
