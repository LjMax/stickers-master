# Stickers Master — Play Store Listing Copy

Copy-paste-ready text for the Google Play Console store listing.
Field length limits are Google's; the character counts below are for the drafted text.

In the Play Console, set one language as the **default** and add the other as a
translation (Store presence → Main store listing → Manage translations).
A Serbian-first market suggests Serbian as the default listing, English as the translation.

### Full description  *(limit: 4000 characters)*
```
Stickers Master helps you complete your sticker album without messy paper checklists.

COLLECTION TRACKING
Mark every sticker as missing, collected, or a duplicate — with an exact count of how many doubles you have. Stickers are organized by group, so the app mirrors the album page by page. Search by sticker code (like ENG2), or filter to show only what you're missing, your duplicates, or the shiny foils.

PAGE SCAN
Take a photo of an album page and tick off everything you've placed in one go — a fast way to update a whole page at once.

SWAP WITH OTHER COLLECTORS
Set your city and the app finds collectors near you whose duplicates match the stickers you still need. Matches are sorted by how many of your missing stickers each person can help with, and people in your own city come first.

CHAT TO ARRANGE TRADES
Send a short message to another collector. If they accept, a private one-on-one chat opens so you can arrange the swap. You get a notification when someone messages you. Block and report tools keep things safe.

SHARE YOUR LIST
Generate a clean "wanted / offered" list and share it anywhere — handy for group chats with friends.

WORKS YOUR WAY
Use it as a guest with no account, or sign in with Google to sync your collection across devices and unlock swapping. Available in Serbian and English, with light and dark themes.

Stickers Master is free, has no ads, and shows sticker codes only — no copyrighted images.

The first supported album is the Panini FIFA World Cup 2026 collection. More albums will follow.

Stickers Master is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Panini, FIFA, or any official album publisher. "Panini" and "FIFA World Cup" are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to describe the album this app helps you track.
```

## Notes

- **App name** is the same in both languages — it's an English name, a decision already locked in.
- The **trademark disclaimer** at the end of each full description is deliberate. It reduces the risk of a Panini/FIFA-driven takedown and is the standard practice for unofficial companion apps.
- The full descriptions are well under the 4000-character limit, leaving room if you want to add anything.
- Play Console also asks for an **app category** (suggest: Tools, or Entertainment) and **tags** — those are picked from menus, not free text.
- Don't put trademarked words in the app name field — it's clean as-is, keep it that way.
